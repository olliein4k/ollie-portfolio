Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who I am

This is the personal website of Ollie Nebel (ollienebel.co.uk). I decide how information collected through it is used. You can contact me at ollie.nebel@gmail.com.

What this site collects

  • Technical data: This site is hosted by Netlify. When you visit, Netlify’s servers automatically log standard information, including your IP address, browser type, the pages you request, and the date and time. I can see summary traffic statistics, such as page views, in Netlify’s dashboard.
  • Emails: If you email me, I receive your email address and your message. My email is provided by Google (Gmail).
  • Browser storage: The news ticker saves two small items in your browser’s local storage: the latest headlines (kept for up to 30 minutes, so the page loads faster) and whether you collapsed the ticker. These stay on your device and I cannot see them. You can clear them in your browser settings.

Third-party services

When you load this site, your browser requests some content directly from other providers. They receive your IP address and browser details as part of that request, and each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) supplies the typefaces.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) supplies the 3D graphics library used in the home page background.
  • A proxy service (go.x2u.in) retrieves the Space.com news feed shown in the ticker. Headline links open Space.com, which has its own privacy policy.

Why I use it

I use technical data to keep the site secure and working, and to see how many people visit and which pages they view. I use emails only to reply to you. The third-party services are needed to display the site. This is based on my legitimate interests in running a secure website and responding to enquiries.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set cookies of its own, run advertising or analytics scripts, or track you across other websites.

Sharing and transfers

Netlify processes the technical data on my behalf and may do so outside the UK, including in the United States, under the safeguards in its privacy policy and data processing terms. The providers listed above may also process data outside the UK. I do not sell your information or share it with anyone else.

How long I keep it

Netlify keeps access logs for a short period, around 30 days. I keep emails only as long as I need to reply, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask what personal data I hold about you, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to how I use it. Contact me using the email above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Other websites

Links on this site, such as LinkedIn, lead to other sites with their own privacy policies.

Changes

I will update this page if the way the site handles information changes.
