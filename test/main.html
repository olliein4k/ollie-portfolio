About

I like problems where analysis, evidence and practical work have to meet. The pattern in my work is the same across fields: build a model, test how the answer changes when the assumptions change, then explain the result to someone who wasn't part of it.

That has meant a DCF and sensitivity analysis, a cash flow model for the Wharton investment competition, flight simulations in OpenRocket, and independent research on hypersonic flow. I'm applying for degree apprenticeships and school-leaver programmes in engineering, finance, consulting and audit, for 2027 entry.

Tools Excel, Python, C#, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, Assembly, OpenRocket, Manim

Projects

  • Modelling hypersonic flight

    Independent 10-page report on whether reusable hypersonic flight is practical, using NASA and ESA literature. I then gave a one-hour lecture to the Moncrieff-Jones Society, from the Navier-Stokes equations to DSMC, and published the work in Quantum Ultimatum, the school science journal.

    Research, fluid dynamics

  • Wharton Global High School Investment Competition

    Data Lead for a team of 6 among more than 5,000 teams. I led the quantitative analysis and dataset management, and built the Excel cash flow model behind our stock recommendations.

    Finance, Excel modelling

  • Buy Now, Pay Later talk and Kelly criterion session

    Presented to the Economics Society on the growth, light regulation and consumer risks of point-of-sale financing. In the Econometrics Club I worked through the derivation of the Kelly criterion for sizing bets and investments.

    Economics, finance

  • RSA cryptography and satellite quantum key distribution

    Researched Euler's totient function and RSA, taught myself Manim, and delivered a 20-minute animated presentation to the Further Mathematics cohort. A follow-on report compared satellites with fibre for quantum key distribution, covering link budgets and atmospheric noise.

    Mathematics, Python, research

  • Bridge design

    Team-built inverted Pratt truss bridge that weighed 108.26g and held 27.5kg, a ratio of about 254 to 1. It won the physics year group competition.

    Structures, optimisation

  • UK Youth Rocketry Challenge

    Five-person team designing, building and launching a rocket to a target altitude of 229 metres. I used OpenRocket for flight modelling, and we manufactured parts by 3D printing and laser cutting. We competed at the regional round in Cambridge.

    Aerospace, simulation

  • CPU built from logic gates

    A Turing-complete CPU built in Turing Complete, with registers, an ALU and a conditional unit. I wrote a custom instruction set and assembly programs, including a maze solver.

    Computer architecture

  • Metroidvania game (in progress)

    Solo Unity and C# project. So far I have built the input and movement systems from scratch on top of Unity's physics and collision engine.

    C#, systems design
